# B2B Cold Email Subject Lines for Manufacturing | Zero Hype

- **"Reducing \[specific production cost\] at \[company size\] manufacturers"** — Operational specificity opens emails here. Cost reduction, downtime reduction, scrap rate — pick one number and name it.
- **"\[Their product category\] supply chain issue — seen this?"** — References a real operational concern in their segment. If the issue is current and accurate, the open rate is high.
- **"How \[named manufacturer\] cut \[X\]% from \[specific process\]"** — A peer result with named company and a specific metric. Manufacturers respect proof over promise.

## Subject Lines That Fail

- **"Transforming your manufacturing operations"** — Transformation language is abstraction. Manufacturing buyers deal in concrete numbers and physical constraints.
- **"Innovative solution for your production line"** — Innovation without specification is noise. What solution? What line? What problem?

One rule: manufacturing buyers trust numbers and distrust adjectives. If your subject line contains a superlative but no metric, rewrite it. Specificity is the entry ticket in this market.
