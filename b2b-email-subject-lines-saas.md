# B2B Cold Email Subject Lines for SaaS | Zero Hype

- **"How \[Competitor Name\] is handling \[specific pain\]"** — Works because it anchors to something real in their world. The buyer opens it to see if the intelligence is accurate. Keep the email short and specific or the trust collapses immediately.
- **"\[Company name\] + \[your product category\] — worth 15 min?"** — Clean, honest, and low-pressure. SaaS buyers appreciate directness. No fake personalization, no false urgency.
- **"\[Specific metric\] improvement at \[similar company\]"** — A named result from a company they recognize pulls opens. Not "10x ROI" — that is noise. A specific number from a specific company is signal.

## Subject Lines That Fail

- **"Quick question"** — Every SaaS rep uses this. It signals template immediately. The buyer has seen it 200 times this year.
- **"Re: following up"** — Fake reply threads feel manipulative and damage trust before the email is read.

One rule worth keeping: specificity beats cleverness every time in SaaS outreach. The buyers are technical, skeptical, and fast. A subject line that makes a precise claim beats one that tries to create intrigue. Earn the open with information, not tricks.
