- **Works council agreement support.** Vendors who have template Betriebsvereinbarungen and experience facilitating the works council process remove a significant procurement obstacle. This is a genuine competitive differentiator in Germany and Austria.
- **German payroll and labour law compliance.** German payroll is complex: Lohnsteuer calculations, Sozialversicherung contributions, Kurzarbeit processing, and Tarifvertrag (collective bargaining agreement) handling. HR tools without native German payroll processing require additional solutions.
- **DATEV integration.** For Mittelstand companies, DATEV integration for payroll data export is often a mandatory requirement rather than a nice-to-have.
- **Employee data DSGVO compliance.** Employee personal data processing requires specific legal bases under DSGVO. HR tools must have clear data processing agreements and support employee data rights (access, rectification, erasure) natively.

## The Evaluation Timeline

DACH HR software evaluations run longer than in other markets — 6-12 months from initial contact to signed contract in Mittelstand and enterprise contexts. Works council involvement, IT security review, and legal review all add time that cannot be shortened by sales pressure.
