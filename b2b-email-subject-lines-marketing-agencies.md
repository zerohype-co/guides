# B2B Cold Email Subject Lines for Marketing Agencies | Zero Hype

- **"Your \[paid/SEO/content\] setup — one thing worth checking"** — Specific channel, low-pressure framing. Agency operators respond to operational observations, not feature lists.
- **"How \[named agency of similar size\] increased margin on retainer clients"** — Margin is the core metric for agencies. Named peer, specific operational outcome.
- **"\[Client category\] campaign performance — benchmark for your agency"** — Benchmark data specific to their client vertical. Agencies want to know how they compare, not how great your product is.

## Subject Lines That Fail

- **"Scale your agency with \[product\]"** — Every agency tool vendor uses this. It signals template immediately to people who recognize templates.
- **"AI-powered solution for marketing agencies"** — An AI claim with no specifics in a subject line reads as noise in 2024. Every vendor has AI now.

One rule: you are selling to people who evaluate messaging for a living. Your subject line is a live demonstration of your ability to communicate clearly. If it is generic, that is the only pitch review they need.
